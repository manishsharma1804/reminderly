2. DETAILED STORE LISTING DESCRIPTION (Copy & Paste to Developer Console)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Reminderly is your intelligent, context-aware productivity and reminder assistant built with Manifest V3. Designed with a modern glassmorphic interface, Reminderly pairs you with Remi, your smart animated assistant, while enforcing deep work focus sessions, blocking distracting websites, and tracking wellness micro-breaks.

🌟 KEY FEATURES:

👦 REMI ASSISTANT
• Animated Reactions: Remi plays a seamless 3-stage reaction sequence (Welcome Wave → Idle Transition → Waiting Loop → Celebration Outro) on active web pages.
• Screen Customization: Set Remi to float at the Top Right or Bottom Right of your screen.
• Smart Browser Fallback: Automatically falls back to native OS desktop notifications when browsing Chrome restricted pages (such as chrome://newtab or Settings).

🧠 SMART CONTEXT AWARENESS & DEEP WORK
• Priority Work Websites: Automatically detects when you are active on work domains like Notion, Figma, GitHub, or LeetCode.
• Flow Protection: Low and Medium priority reminders are held silently in the queue while you are in flow state.
• High Priority Overrides: Medicine and critical task alerts always trigger immediately.

🛡️ FOCUS MODE & DISTRACTING WEBSITE BLOCKER
• Flexible Focus Sessions: Quick launch 25-minute (Pomodoro), 45-minute, or Custom minute focus timers.
• Distracting Site Interceptor: Blocks distracting websites (YouTube, Reddit, Instagram, etc.) with a sleek full-page glassmorphic blocker modal during focus sessions.

🏥 HEALTH HUB & WELLNESS MICRO-BREAKS
• Hydration Tracker: Log daily water glasses (+1 Glass quick log) with smart interval alerts.
• 20-20-20 Eye Rest & Posture Checks: Micro-break timers to prevent eye strain and physical fatigue.
• Menstrual Cycle Tracker: 100% private, offline period predictions and pre-period reminders.
• Auto Health Sync: Health break schedules automatically sync with your active reminders list.

🔒 100% OFFLINE & PRIVATE
• Local Browser Storage: All reminders, profile data, focus stats, and water logs remain strictly on your local browser (chrome.storage.local).
• Zero Telemetry: No analytics, tracking scripts, cookies, or external server communications.
